Privacy & Cookie Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Doorstep Tailor (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect and how we use it, in line with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

1. Who we are

Doorstep Tailor, London, is the data controller. Contact: [hello@doorsteptailor.co.uk]

2. What we collect

When you book or contact us: your name, phone number, email, address and postcode, preferred appointment times, details of the garments to be altered, and your measurements. We also keep records of orders and payments.

3. Why we use it

To arrange fittings, pickups and deliveries, carry out your alterations, take payment, and contact you about your order. Our lawful basis is performance of a contract with you. We keep financial records to meet our legal obligations.

4. Marketing

We will only send you offers or news if you have agreed to it. You can opt out at any time.

5. Sharing

We never sell your data. We share it only with trusted providers who help us run the service, such as booking, messaging, payment and website hosting providers, and only as needed.

6. How long we keep it

Order and contact details are kept for up to 2 years after your last order, and financial records for 6 years as required by law. Measurements are kept so we can serve you again, unless you ask us to delete them.

7. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, object to its use, or ask us to restrict it. Contact us using the details above. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

8. Cookies

Our website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser may store small, essential settings so the site works properly. If we add analytics in future, we will ask for your consent first and update this policy.
