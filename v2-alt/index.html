      Questions

      Good to know

      How do I request a quote?

      Choose a category, tap the alterations you need, add photos and instructions, and send. We'll call you to discuss it.

      Are prices fixed?

      Published prices are reference prices. The final cost may depend on fabric, construction and finish, and is always agreed with you before work starts.

      Do you collect?

      Yes. We currently offer collection and return in Chelsea. There is no shop to visit.

      Can you alter designer or bridal garments?

      Yes. Send photos and details for an assessment and a personal quotation.

      Do you repair curtains and household fabrics?

      Yes. Choose Curtains & Household, then add photos, dimensions and your instructions.

      Do I need a fitting?

      Some alterations need a fitting or a closer look before the final quote. We'll tell you when we call.
