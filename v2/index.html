Need to know

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I receive a quote?

Choose your service, upload photographs and explain the work. We will contact you to discuss the quotation.

Are the prices fixed?

Prices shown are reference prices. Final cost may depend on fabric, construction, finish and complexity.

Can you work on designer or bridal garments?

Please send clear photographs and details. We will assess the garment and confirm whether the requested work is suitable.

Do you repair curtains and household fabrics?

Yes. Select Household Fabrics and include photographs, dimensions and your instructions.

Do you collect?

Yes. We collect and return across Chelsea. There is no shop to visit.

Do I need a fitting?

You can start by sending photographs online. Some work may need a fitting or a closer look before the final quote. We will tell you when we call.
