    Need to know

    Frequently Asked Questions

    How do I get a quote?

    Choose your alterations, take photos and send them to us on WhatsApp with your postcode. We'll call you to confirm the quote.

    Are the prices fixed?

    Our price list is for reference only. The final cost depends on the fabric, construction and finish, and is always agreed with you before work begins.

    Do you offer discounts?

    Yes. Regular customers and repeat or larger jobs can be discounted. Just ask when we call.

    Do you collect?

    Yes. We collect and return across Chelsea. There is no shop to visit.

    Can you work on designer or bridal garments?

    Yes. Send clear photographs and details, and we'll give you a personal quotation.

    Do you repair curtains and household fabrics?

    Yes. Send photographs, dimensions and your instructions.

    Do I need a fitting?

    Some alterations need a fitting or a closer look before the final quote. We'll tell you when we call.
